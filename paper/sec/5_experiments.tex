\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
\emph{T-LESS}~\cite{hodan2017tless}: 30 texture-less industrial objects with discrete and continuous symmetries, heavy clutter and
occlusion. We train on the PBR and real training images and test on the BOP19 targets of the real test set. \emph{YCB-V}~\cite{xiang2018posecnn}:
21 household objects in cluttered video frames; PBR and real training images, BOP19 test targets.
\emph{SPEED+}~\cite{park2022speedplus}: a single spacecraft; we train only on the synthetic images and test on the full
hardware-in-the-loop \emph{lightbox} and \emph{sunlamp} sets, without using their images in any form during training.
\emph{SYMSOL-T}~\cite{hsiao2024liepose}: five symmetric shapes (tetrahedron, cube, icosahedron, cone, cylinder) with random rotations
and translations; LiePose's training and test splits.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
On T-LESS and YCB-V we report the BOP average recalls of VSD, MSSD and MSPD and their mean AR~\cite{hodan2020bop}, computed with the
BOP toolkit; VSD uses the toolkit's Python renderer (\cref{sec:supp-protocol}). On SPEED+ we report the mean translation error
$e_t$ (m), the mean rotation error $e_q=2\arccos|\langle q,\hat q\rangle|$ (deg), and the official SPEC2021 score
$S=\tfrac{1}{n}\sum(e_q^{\mathrm{rad}}+e_t/\|t\|)$ with the competition's precision thresholds~\cite{park2022speedplus}, on the full test
sets. On SYMSOL-T we report the mean symmetric rotation error (deg) and mean translation error per shape, as in LiePose.

\paragraph{Baselines and training budget.}
The main baseline is LiePose~\cite{hsiao2024liepose}, which we retrain on every benchmark with its public code. All models we train
see the same number of training images as LiePose: 400k steps with batch 64 on T-LESS, YCB-V and SPEED+, and 400k steps with batch 32
on SYMSOL-T (LiePose: 800k steps with batch 16). Because every noisy pose of \method queries the world model, we use 64 noisy poses per
image instead of LiePose's 256; the control uses the same 64. We evaluate the final checkpoint of every run; no checkpoint is chosen on
test data. For each run we sample with the weight average (EMA) or the live weights, whichever has the lower training loss, decided
before any test evaluation (\cref{sec:supp-repro}). Published numbers of other methods are copied from their papers and marked by
protocol.

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The world model has 6 blocks of width 384 and is trained for 30k steps (20k on SYMSOL-T) with AdamW. The denoiser has 4 blocks of width
256. Sampling uses LiePose's 100-step sampler. Full details are in \cref{sec:supp-impl}.
