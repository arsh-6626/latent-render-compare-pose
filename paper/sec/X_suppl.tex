\section{Implementation details}
\label{sec:supp-impl}

\paragraph{Observation encoding.}
Crops of $224\times224$ pixels are produced by LiePose's data pipeline, including its training-time augmentation (dynamic zoom-in, colour,
blur and noise), and are exported once; DINOv2 ViT-S/14 (frozen, ImageNet normalisation) encodes them into $16\times16$ patch tokens
that are average-pooled $2\times2$ to 64 tokens of 384 dimensions and stored in half precision. Foreground weights are the visible
mask, max-pooled to the token grid and dilated by one token. On SPEED+, two augmented copies of every synthetic training image are
exported; on T-LESS, 180,224 augmented training crops are drawn from LiePose's training set (PBR and real images).
\TODO{YCB-V numbers of exported crops.}

\paragraph{World model.}
\Cref{tab:supp-hparams} lists the hyperparameters. The candidate set per training crop, the reference noise and the loss are described
in \cref{sec:supp-wm}.

\paragraph{Denoiser.}
Token transformer of width 256, depth 4, 8 heads, MLP ratio 4, adaLN modulation with zero-initialised gates in every block. Input
tokens: LayerNorm then a linear map of the concatenation $[\zobs_p,\zhat_p,\zhat_p-\zobs_p,w_p]$ ($3\cdot384+1$ dimensions), plus a learned
position embedding. The conditioning token is the sum of Fourier embeddings of the step and of the 6-dimensional Lie-algebra coordinates
of the noisy pose and a linear embedding of the crop features (log zoom, normalised crop centre, and on SPEED+ the log box aspect),
followed by SiLU and a linear layer. The output head maps the final conditioning token to the 6-dimensional noise.

\paragraph{Diffusion, optimisation, sampling.}
The generative process is LiePose's SE(3) diffusion formulation, run with its public code and unchanged; it is not a contribution of
this paper, and it is shared by \method, the control and the LiePose baseline, so that they differ only in the denoiser. As LiePose:
SE(3) parametrisation with tangent-space representation, 100 noise levels with a variance-exploding power-law schedule, noise-prediction loss,
Adam with learning rate $10^{-4}$ and exponential decay to $10^{-5}$ over the second half of training, weight averaging with decay
0.999 every 5 steps, and the 100-step sampler with the same options as LiePose's test script. The world-model parameters are restored
after every optimiser step, so that weight decay cannot move them.

\section{World-model training}
\label{sec:supp-wm}

\paragraph{Candidates.}
For each training crop with pose $g^\star=(R^\star,t^\star)$, a batch row contains: $g^\star$; 11 rotations drawn uniformly on SO(3) with
$t^\star$; 12 rotations obtained by rotating $R^\star$ by a random axis and an angle up to $30^\circ$, with $t^\star$; 4 copies of $R^\star$ with
$t^\star$ perturbed by Gaussian noise (scaled per axis to the range of the translation parametrisation); 4 copies of $R^\star$ with
translations of other training crops. The rotation distance of each candidate to the ground truth is the minimum over the object's
symmetric equivalents.

\paragraph{Reference.}
One reference per row, retrieved at a noisy copy of the ground truth and never from the row's own source image (exclusion is by source
image, so augmented copies of the same image are excluded together). All candidates of the row share the reference, as the samples of
one image share retrievals at nearby poses during diffusion.

\paragraph{Loss.}
\Cref{eq:wmloss}: cross-entropy between the softmax of negative energies over the candidates and the soft target built from the
candidate distances, plus the energy at the ground truth. The temperature is a learned scalar initialised at $e^{-2}$.

\paragraph{SYMSOL-T extras.}
On SYMSOL-T the world model is trained from scratch on crops, and optionally with two sets of extra regression targets under the
weighted-energy loss: in-plane rotations of training images (exact poses obtained by rotating the image), and pairs rendered with the
SYMSOL-T renderer at small pose offsets from training poses. The rendered pairs are additional synthetic data that LiePose does not use
(\cref{sec:supp-disclose}). Four variants are trained for 30k steps (retrieval only; + geometry tokens; + rolls and pairs; + both),
and one is selected on held-out training images (\cref{sec:supp-disclose}); \cref{tab:abl-wm} reports all four.
\TODO{name the selected variant.}

\paragraph{Checkpoint selection.}
T-LESS: 3\% of the training source images are held out of the bank and of training; the checkpoint with the lowest selection error on
500 of them is kept, and test data are evaluated once. SPEED+: the same criterion on the synthetic validation split. SYMSOL-T: the final
step of each variant, scored on held-out training images (\cref{sec:supp-disclose}).

\section{Geometry tokens}
\label{sec:supp-geo}

Let $K$ be the camera intrinsics and $(s,x_0,y_0)$ the zoom and origin of a crop, so that a crop pixel is $s(u-(x_0,y_0))$. A pose
$(R,t)$ in LiePose's parametrisation is mapped to the camera frame with the crop box (as in LiePose's translation recovery), including
the conversion from allocentric to egocentric rotation. For the reference token $p$ with crop-pixel centre $c_p$, we back-project its
image position onto the plane $Z=Z^{\mathrm{ref}}$ at the reference object depth, $P_p$, express it in the object frame,
$o_p=(R^{\mathrm{ref}})^\top(P_p-t^{\mathrm{ref}})$, move it to the target pose, $P'_p=Ro_p+t$, and project it into the observation's crop. The
token receives $[\,c_p/112-1,\ (\pi_k(P'_p)-c_p)/\Delta,\ \log(Z'_p/Z^{\mathrm{ref}})\,]$, with $\Delta$ the token size in pixels, Fourier-encoded at
frequencies $2^{-2},\dots,2^{6}$ and added through a zero-initialised MLP. The plane approximation is exact for the object centre and
first-order for nearby surface points; it is only an input, never a metric. The seven virtual points are the object centre and the
six points at $\pm r$ along the object axes, with $r$ a nominal radius (half the shape size on SYMSOL-T; the largest keypoint radius on
SPEED+); their projections at both poses, their image displacement and both depths form seven tokens with learned identity embeddings.
Lens distortion is ignored.

\section{Evaluation protocols}
\label{sec:supp-protocol}

\paragraph{BOP (T-LESS, YCB-V).}
We use LiePose's test dataset on the BOP19 test targets and its evaluator, which writes the BOP CSV and calls the BOP toolkit. The
toolkit's C++ renderer was not available in our environment, so VSD is computed with the toolkit's Python (vispy) renderer; this
changes only how the depth maps used by VSD are rendered. MSSD and MSPD do not use a renderer. \TODO{state the number of test
instances per dataset.}

\paragraph{SPEED+.}
Estimates are converted from LiePose's crop-relative parametrisation to the camera frame with the estimated translation and the crop
box. For each image, $e_q=2\arccos|\langle q,\hat q\rangle|$ and $e_t=\|t-\hat t\|$. The score is $S_q+S_t$ with
$S_q=e_q^{\mathrm{rad}}$ set to zero when $e_q<0.169^\circ$ and $S_t=e_t/\|t\|$ set to zero when $e_t/\|t\|<2.173$\,mm/m, using the
SPEC2021 thresholds~\cite{park2022speedplus}; each component is thresholded on its own, and the reported score, rotation part and
translation part are means over all images of the lightbox and sunlamp sets. The SPEED+ paper~\cite{park2022speedplus} states the
thresholds as zeroing the pose score only when both components are below them. \TODO{check which rule the official SPEC2021 scoring

\section{Reproducing LiePose and choosing weights}
\label{sec:supp-repro}

We retrained LiePose with its public code on every benchmark. On T-LESS our run uses batch 64 instead of the paper's 32 (400k steps
in both cases), and our models are matched to our run. LiePose's test script samples with the exponential moving average (EMA) of the
weights. For our T-LESS checkpoint, the EMA weights differ from the live weights by a small relative amount but give a much higher
training loss than the live weights, and sampling with them fails; sampling with the live weights reproduces the published accuracy.
We therefore choose, for every checkpoint we report (LiePose, control and \method, on every benchmark), between EMA and live weights by
the training loss on fresh training batches, before any test evaluation. \Cref{tab:supp-ema} lists the losses and the choices.

\section{Information audit}
\label{sec:supp-disclose}

\paragraph{At test time.}
(i) Crops from ground-truth boxes on all benchmarks (\cref{sec:protocol}): visible box and visible mask on T-LESS and YCB-V, as in
LiePose; the box of the keypoints projected with the ground-truth pose on SPEED+; the box of the rendered foreground on SYMSOL-T, where
LiePose uses the full image. (ii) The object identity, used to choose the reference bank. (iii) Camera intrinsics, for geometry tokens.
(iv) Tokens, poses and boxes of training crops, in the bank. No test image is in the bank, and no ground-truth pose is used by the
model; ground truth is read only by the scorers and by rows and figures marked as diagnostics (oracle selection, oracle tokens, flip
timing, world-model discrimination).

\paragraph{During training.}
Only training images: PBR and real training images on T-LESS and YCB-V, synthetic images on SPEED+ (the unlabeled lightbox and sunlamp
images are not used in any form), and LiePose's training split on SYMSOL-T, plus the in-plane rotations of these images and the rendered
pose pairs used by the SYMSOL-T world model (\cref{sec:supp-wm}).

\paragraph{Model selection.}
Denoisers: final checkpoint, EMA or live weights by training loss (\cref{sec:supp-repro}). World models: held-out training images on
T-LESS, the synthetic validation split on SPEED+, and on SYMSOL-T 3\% of the training images (1,350 per shape, none of them a source
of the rolls or rendered pairs), which are removed from the world model's training set and reference bank. The selection score is the
refinement pick error on 500 of these images (the energy's choice among 24 rotation perturbations of 0.25--8$^\circ$ around the
ground truth); the variant with the lowest score at its final step is used, a rule fixed before training. Test images were not used to
train or select any of the four variants. However, the variants themselves were designed during an earlier development phase in which
the same diagnostic was computed on 500 test images; we disclose this, and \cref{tab:abl-wm} reports every variant on the test set.

\paragraph{Deviations from LiePose's setup.}
64 instead of 256 noisy poses per training image for the control and \method; batch 64 for our T-LESS reproduction of LiePose
(paper: 32); weights chosen by training loss (\cref{sec:supp-repro}); VSD rendered with the Python renderer. The control and
\method train on a fixed set of pre-augmented crops whose tokens are cached (180,224 crops on T-LESS, two augmented copies of each
synthetic image on SPEED+), whereas LiePose draws a new augmentation every time an image is used.

\paragraph{Broader impact.}
Pose estimation of known objects supports robotic manipulation and autonomous spacecraft rendezvous. We see no specific risk beyond
those of perception systems in general; for safety-critical use, the failure modes in \cref{fig:qual} and the reliance on
ground-truth crops (\cref{sec:conclusion}) must be addressed first.
